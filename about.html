About e-Receipts

Digital receipts that remain checkable.

e-Receipts is built around a simple idea: a receipt should remain useful after the sale and should be possible to verify.

e-Receipts is a product of kilimo.tech.

e-Receipts is built by kilimo.tech, a Kenyan software development company.

kilimo.tech builds software products and develops software for businesses. e-Receipts is its platform for issuing and verifying digital receipts.

A receipt that never gets printed.

Every paper receipt is thermal paper that came from a tree and usually ends up in the bin the same day. Because an e-Receipt is a link, the paper is simply never used.

At the scale of a business's daily sales, that adds up — and it aligns with the UN Sustainable Development Goal on responsible consumption and production (SDG 12).
