Features

What e-Receipts does.

A digital receipt for every sale: the customer keeps it, the business records it, and anyone can check it.

A receipt that can be verified.

Anyone with the link can confirm the receipt was issued by the stated business and see if the sale was later cancelled.

See Verifiable Receipts →

A link the customer keeps.

Opens in a browser without an app — kept for returns, warranties and expense records.

See Customer Receipts →

A receipts book that writes itself.

Every receipt issued is recorded in the merchant workspace under the business's own identity.

See Merchant Records →

Works with or without a POS.

Issue receipts directly, or connect an existing sales system to issue them automatically.

See Standalone Receipts →

Built to be integrated.

A POS or business system sends the sale and gets back a receipt link for the customer.

See Integrations →
