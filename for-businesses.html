For businesses

Give your customers a receipt they can keep.

e-Receipts lets a business issue a digital receipt for every sale and keep the issued receipts in its merchant workspace.

Get started

You do not need to replace your POS.

If you already have a sales system, connect it to e-Receipts.

If you do not, you can use e-Receipts on its own.

Your customer gets a link.

The receipt opens without an app. The customer can keep the link and return to it later.

The receipt can be checked.

Anyone with the receipt link can confirm that it was issued by the stated business.
