Verification

The receipt proves where it came from.

Open an e-Receipt and the system confirms that it was issued by the named business.

If the business later cancels the sale, the receipt does not disappear. It is marked as cancelled.

See verification →
For customers

The customer keeps the receipt.

The customer receives a link that can be opened without installing an app.

That link can be kept for a return, warranty claim, expense record or any other reason the receipt may be needed later.

For customers →
For businesses

Every receipt becomes part of the business record.

Each receipt issued by the merchant is kept in the merchant workspace.

The receipt remains under the business's own identity even when the business changes its name.

For businesses →
Integration

Use it on its own or connect it to your POS.

A business can issue receipts directly through e-Receipts.

A point-of-sale system can also send sales to e-Receipts and issue a receipt automatically. Kijiduka, another kilimo.tech product, can use e-Receipts for its sales.
